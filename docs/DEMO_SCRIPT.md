# PARAMPARA AI — Judge Demonstration Script (4–5 Minutes)

**Core Demonstration Thesis:**  
*"Most platforms show the craft. PARAMPARA AI preserves the knowledge behind it, keeps the artisan connected to that knowledge, and makes it responsibly discoverable."*

---

## Act 1 — The Human Problem (0:00 - 0:30)
- **Visual:** Landing Page Hero.
- **Narrative:** Traditional Indian craft knowledge is vulnerable because it lives in oral memories, family workshops, and scattered documents. Generic AI systems either hallucinate cultural facts or detach the artisan from the craft. E-commerce sites treat craft merely as merchandise.
- **Action:** Highlight the mission banner: *"Every craft carries a story. Let's make sure it survives."* Note the two clear paths: **Explore Heritage** and **Preserve a Story**.

---

## Act 2 — Voice of Tradition: Artisan Documentation (0:30 - 1:30)
- **Visual:** Artisan Studio -> Voice of Tradition (`/studio/stories/new`).
- **Narrative:** Artisans often struggle with typing long English descriptions. We provide a voice-first documentation studio.
- **Action:**
  1. Click **"Record Oral History"** or select a sample recording in Hindi/Telugu/Gujarati.
  2. Show Gemini 3.5 Transcribe generating a draft transcript.
  3. **Point out the differentiator:** The UI clearly labels it as an *AI Draft* and highlights localized dialect terms (e.g. *Taaseer*, *Kile ki Mitti*) for manual contributor verification.
  4. Show the **Consent & Attribution Panel**: The artisan explicitly selects named attribution, research scope, and public visibility.
  5. Click **"Submit for Curator Review"**.

---

## Act 3 — Curator Review Console & Claim-Level Truth (1:30 - 2:20)
- **Visual:** Curator Console (`/curator/queue`).
- **Narrative:** How do we prevent AI hallucinations from poisoning the cultural record? Human-in-the-loop claim-level verification.
- **Action:**
  1. Open the pending submission from the Tarkashi Guild.
  2. Point out how **Contributor Facts** are cleanly separated from **AI Suggestions**.
  3. Show the **Claim Verification Panel**: Demonstrate clicking a claim and changing its status from `source_attached` to `curator_reviewed`.
  4. Show the attached GI registry citation and click **"Approve Record for Public Archive"**.
  5. Show the live **Audit History** logging the curator's action.

---

## Act 4 — Heritage Lens: Responsible Visual Discovery (2:20 - 3:05)
- **Visual:** Heritage Lens (`/heritage-lens`).
- **Narrative:** Visitors can upload a photograph of an unfamiliar craft artifact.
- **Action:**
  1. Upload a photograph of Bidriware.
  2. Show Gemini 3.8 Flash multimodal analysis returning visible features (blackened zinc substrate, pure silver wire inlay, Persian arabesque).
  3. Show the candidate matches ranked against our documented pilot archive.
  4. **Key Safeguard Proof:** Point out the visible banner: *"AI-assisted suggestion; not an authenticity certificate. Visual analysis does not prove antiquity or community ownership."*
  5. Click the matching candidate to open the full digital heritage vault record.

---

## Act 5 — Heritage Q&A: Grounded RAG with Citable Sources (3:05 - 3:55)
- **Visual:** Craft Detail Page (`/explore/bidriware`) -> Heritage Q&A Drawer.
- **Narrative:** Visitors can ask questions in natural language.
- **Action:**
  1. Ask: *"Why is Bidar Fort soil used for blackening the metal?"*
  2. Show the grounded answer explaining the chemical nitrate reaction, accompanied by **clickable citations** to the National Crafts Museum and GI Registry.
  3. Ask an out-of-domain question: *"How do you construct a blockchain smart contract?"*
  4. **Crucial Proof:** Show the system refuse to hallucinate: *"The verified archive does not currently contain documented records... our system explicitly refrains from generating ungrounded claims."*

  3. Navigate to **Opportunity Connect**: Show verified government fellowships (Ministry of Textiles ₹3.6 Lakh grant) and museum exhibitions with closing dates and direct application links.
  4. View the public artisan profile for **Shah Rashid Ahmed Qadri** showing verified badge, bio, products, and direct ethical enquiry link.
